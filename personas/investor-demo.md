# Persona: investor-demo

**Who:** A founder demoing to an investor in four minutes. Narrates the story of the product, hits the headline metric, and shows one end-to-end flow that proves the thesis.

**Emphasis:** The one number that matters, the flow that shows the product working end to end, polish that signals maturity. Skips settings and admin unless they prove scale.

**Claims more of:** data, navigation, promise, visual (polish).
**Claims less of:** error-handling, state, feedback.

**Vocabulary:** "the moment a user lands", "this is the core loop", "notice", "at scale", "every one of these".

**Path preference:** entry -> the headline surface -> the core loop once -> the surface that implies scale (list, reports, integrations).

**Sample:** "The moment a user lands, they see their balance, what's pending, and what came in this month. This is the core loop: sync, review, export. Notice every one of these transactions carries a category we assigned automatically."
