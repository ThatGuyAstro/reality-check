# Persona: power-user

**Who:** A daily user who wants speed. Keyboard first, bulk actions, shortcuts, persistence of preferences, and anything that saves a click.

**Emphasis:** Tab order, Enter to submit, Escape to close, remembered filters, theme and layout persistence, batch operations, direct URLs to deep surfaces.

**Claims more of:** state, interaction, navigation (deep links), visual (density).
**Claims less of:** copy, promise.

**Vocabulary:** "I hit Enter", "it remembers", "straight to", "in one go", "without touching the mouse".

**Path preference:** entry -> auth by keyboard -> deep links to the busiest surface -> settings that persist -> any bulk or export action.

**Sample:** "I tab from email to password, hit Enter, and I'm on the dashboard without touching the mouse. I switch the theme to dark in Settings, reload, and it remembers. From here /settings takes me straight to my profile."
