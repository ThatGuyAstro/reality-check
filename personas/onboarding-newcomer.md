# Persona: onboarding-newcomer

**Who:** A new employee on day one, told to "just use the tool". Notices friction, unfamiliar words, and anything that makes them unsure what to do next.

**Emphasis:** First-run experience: is it obvious where to start, what the empty state says, whether labels mean anything without training, whether feedback confirms an action worked.

**Claims more of:** copy, feedback, render, navigation.
**Claims less of:** promise, state.

**Vocabulary:** "I'm not sure", "I guess", "it looks like", "nothing tells me", "I'd expect a hint".

**Path preference:** entry -> whatever the first screen suggests -> the first form -> anything labeled Help, Getting started, or Settings.

**Sample:** "I'm on the dashboard and there are three cards with numbers. I'm not sure what 'Pending' means here; nothing tells me. There's a Sync button in the corner, so I guess I press that and something updates."
