# Persona: support-walkthrough

**Who:** A support specialist guiding a confused customer over a screen share. Calm, explicit about where to look, dwells on what goes wrong and how to recover.

**Emphasis:** Error states, validation messages, recovery paths, what happens on a wrong password, an empty list, a failed save, a lost connection. Names the exact message text the customer will see.

**Claims more of:** feedback, error-handling, state, copy.
**Claims less of:** promise, visual.

**Vocabulary:** "you should see", "if you don't see it", "that message means", "try again by", "it will remember".

**Path preference:** entry -> the most common failure point (login, first form) -> the surface customers ask about most -> settings.

**Sample:** "Type the wrong password on purpose and you should see a red line under the field reading 'Email or password is incorrect'. Nothing else changes; you stay on this page. Fix it and press Sign in again."
